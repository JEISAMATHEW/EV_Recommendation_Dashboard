🚗⚡ Excited to share my latest project: EV Charging Recommendation Dashboard!

As part of my learning journey with @upGrad (Offline Xperience), I built an end-to-end Excel-based analytics project that helps EV drivers find the best charging station — not just the nearest one.

The problem: EV drivers often pick a charging station based on distance alone, which can mean long queues or a station that's out of reach given their current battery level.

What I built:
🔋 A weighted recommendation engine scoring stations on Distance (40%), Queue Length (30%), and Battery Level (30%)
🧹 Cleaned a raw dataset of 1,547 charging stations down to 1,117 reliable records (deduped, fixed lat/long errors, standardized casing)
📊 An interactive dashboard with Top-5 station rankings, Reachable vs Non-Reachable breakdown, and State/City filters
🎯 A clear end-to-end workflow: Raw Data → Cleaning → Scoring → Recommendations → Dashboard

Impact: This kind of approach can help EV drivers save search time, avoid long waits, and make smarter charging decisions — and could extend to real-time queue data, live availability, and even ML-based demand prediction in the future.
